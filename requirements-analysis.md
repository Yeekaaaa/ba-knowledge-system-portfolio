# Requirements Analysis

## Purpose

Turn business problems into structured, testable, implementable requirements.

## Inputs

- Stakeholder goals and decision questions
- Current process and pain points
- Data sources and reporting needs
- Constraints (policy, timeline, systems)

## Steps

1. Clarify business objective and success measures.
2. Identify stakeholders and decision users — [[stakeholder-analysis]].
3. Capture current-state process — [[process-mapping]].
4. Separate business requirements from solution design choices.
5. Define data fields, rules, exceptions, validation.
6. Write acceptance criteria — [[user-stories]].
7. Confirm with stakeholders.
8. Trace requirement → deliverable → test → business value.

## Outputs

- Business requirement summary
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Data dictionary / field rules
- Process maps
- Dashboard or reporting specification

## Common mistakes

- Writing features before defining the business problem
- Mixing assumptions with confirmed facts
- No acceptance criteria or approval owner
- Metrics not linked to decisions

## Example (SYNTHETIC)

Northline Retail Group (fictional): defined monthly import template, validation rules, KPI calculations, and dashboard views for regional margin and budget variance. See raw note: `sample-raw/synthetic-project-note.md`.

## Interview phrasing

> I clarify the decision the requirement supports, map the current workflow, define data and KPI rules, then convert them into testable stories and acceptance criteria.
