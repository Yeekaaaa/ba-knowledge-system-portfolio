# Digital Transformation BA

## Why this domain matters

Organisations run continuous digital and AI change programmes. BAs translate **domain goals** into requirements, operating model changes, data products, and adoption plans.

## Core responsibilities

- Scope transformation domains (journeys, processes, functions)
- Link requirements to value measures
- Support cross-functional delivery teams
- Define data and integration needs
- Plan adoption, training, and change evidence
- Track KPIs: value creation, team health, change progress

- Domain transformation brief

- KPI and benefits framework
- Adoption and training outline
- RAID log (risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies)

## Interview phrasing

> I work on digital transformation from the business side: clarifying the domain outcome, defining requirements and process changes, and making sure we measure value and adoption — not only release dates.
