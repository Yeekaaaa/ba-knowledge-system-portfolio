## Key takeaways

- Experimental evidence suggests voice AI ordering may shift consumers toward more indulgent choices vs human staff.
- Proposed mechanism: cognitive depletion increasing affect-driven decisions.
- Visual avatars may mitigate effects via social presence.
- Industry adoption of drive-thru voice AI is growing alongside accuracy concerns and mixed customer feedback.
