## Story 1 — SYNTHETIC retail reporting requirements

### Competencies

- requirements analysis
- process mapping
- stakeholder communication
- dashboard requirements

### STAR

**Situation:** A fictional multi-store retail group (SYNTHETIC) lacked consistent monthly performance reporting.

**Task:** Define a repeatable reporting workflow and dashboard requirements for regional leadership.

**Action:** Mapped current-state spreadsheet consolidation, drafted KPI dictionary rules, specified import validation, and wrote acceptance criteria for dashboard totals.

**Result:** Produced a structured requirements package for demonstration purposes — not deployed production outcomes.

### Best for

- Tell me about a BA project
- How do you gather requirements?
- How do you define dashboard requirements?
