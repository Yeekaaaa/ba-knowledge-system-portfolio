# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

Identify who is affected by a change, what they need, and how to engage them through analysis and delivery.

## Inputs

- Project scope and domain — [[digital-transformation-framework]]
- Organisational chart or role list
- Known pain points and decision rights
- Change type (process, system, automation)

## Steps

1. **Identify** stakeholders: users, sponsors, approvers, data owners, impacted teams.
2. **Analyse** interest, influence, attitude (support / neutral / resist).
3. **Map** on power/interest or RACI grid.
4. **Define** engagement approach per group (consult, collaborate, inform).
5. **Validate** map with sponsor.
6. **Update** as scope changes.

## Outputs

- Stakeholder register
- Stakeholder map / matrix
- Engagement plan
- RACI for requirements approval and UAT sign-off

## Common mistakes

- Only interviewing IT, not business decision-makers
- Treating "users" as one homogeneous group
- No plan for impacted employees during automation — see [[change-impact-analysis]]
