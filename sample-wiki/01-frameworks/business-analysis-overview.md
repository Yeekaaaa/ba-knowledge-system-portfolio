# Business Analysis Overview

## What it is

Business Analysis is the practice of enabling change by defining needs and recommending solutions that deliver value to stakeholders. In job-market terms, BAs **review processes**, **define requirements**, **bridge business and technology**, and **communicate solutions** back to the organisation.

Public career guidance (Australia) describes BAs as working across IT and other departments to map requirements and improve efficiency. See [[job-market-signal-business-analyst-summary]].

## Why a BA uses a structured framework

A framework turns scattered tasks into recognisable capability areas:

| Capability area | Sample activities |
|---|---|
| Planning & monitoring | Scope analysis work, stakeholder plan |
| Elicitation & collaboration | Interviews, workshops, observation |
| Requirements analysis | Structure, validate, trace requirements |
| Strategy analysis | Link solutions to business outcomes |
| Solution evaluation | Assess performance, gaps, value |
| Requirements life cycle | Manage changes, versions, approvals |

Aligns with IIBA BABOK® areas (study reference — not a claim of certification).

## Core concepts

- **Business need** before solution feature
- **Stakeholder** identification and communication
- **Current state / future state** process thinking
- **Testable requirements** with acceptance criteria
- **Traceability** from need → requirement → test → outcome
