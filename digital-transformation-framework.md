# Digital Transformation Framework

## What it is

Digital transformation is the **continuous rewiring** of how an organisation operates — deploying technology at scale to improve customer experience and reduce cost. It differs from one-off change programmes because technology and expectations keep evolving.

Concepts paraphrased from a public McKinsey explainer. See [[public-article-digital-transformation-summary]].

## Why a BA uses it

Digital BA roles require translating **domain goals** into requirements, data rules, process changes, and adoption evidence — not feature lists alone.

## Core concepts

### Domain over use case

Scope whole domains (e.g. monthly performance reporting) rather than isolated features.

### Problem before technology

Start with the business problem; resist tool-first pilots that do not scale.

### Six capability areas (paraphrased)

1. Value-focused strategy and road map
2. In-house digital talent working with business
3. Scalable operating model (cross-functional teams)
4. Distributed technology (APIs, cloud, developer tooling)
5. Governed, accessible data products
6. Adoption and change management

### Build / change investment ratio

Plan comparable effort for **training, process change, and adoption** as for solution development (rule of thumb cited in source summary).

### Transformation KPI themes

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Value creation | Operational KPIs tied to financial benefit |
| Team health | Ways of working, capability, staffing |
| Change progress | Adoption, engagement, capability building |
