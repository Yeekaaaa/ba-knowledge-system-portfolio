## Core concepts (paraphrased)

- Digital transformation is a **long-term rewiring** of how an organisation operates, deploying technology at scale for customer experience and cost advantage.
- Unlike one-off business transformations, digital change is **continuous** as technology evolves.
- Successful programmes focus on **domains** (journeys, processes, functions) rather than isolated use cases.
- Digital transformation should align with **AI transformation** when AI affects decision-making.
- Start with the **business problem**, not the technology.
- Major capability areas include: value-focused strategy, in-house digital talent, scalable operating model, distributed technology (e.g. APIs, cloud), accessible governed data, and **adoption / change management**.
- Rule of thumb cited: plan comparable investment in **change and adoption** as in solution build.
- Transformation KPI themes: **value creation**, **team health**, **change-management progress**.

## BA-relevant concepts

- Domain scoping
- Cross-functional delivery
- Benefits tracking and value realisation
- Adoption and training requirements
- Product / platform operating models

## Practical implications (interpretation)

BAs in digital transformation roles translate domain goals into requirements, process changes, data rules, and adoption evidence — not only feature lists.

## Limitations

- Enterprise-scale framing; adapt honestly for smaller programmes.
