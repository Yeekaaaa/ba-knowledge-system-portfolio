# Public paper metadata — Voice AI and consumer food choice

> **Copyright:** Citation metadata and original summary only. Publisher PDF is **not** included.

## Citation

Peng, Z. (L.), Yu, H., & Mattila, A. (2026). From human to AI: Understanding the impact of voice AI on consumers' food choices. *International Journal of Hospitality Management*, 134, 104537. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhm.2025.104537

## Topic (one line)

How voice-based AI ordering interfaces may change consumer food choices compared with human staff.

## Summary (own words)

Experimental hospitality research reported that consumers interacting with voice AI were more likely to choose indulgent food options than when ordering from human employees. The proposed mechanism involves greater cognitive depletion during AI interaction, leading to more affect-driven decisions. Visual avatars may reduce this effect by adding social presence. Industry context includes expanding drive-thru voice AI adoption alongside concerns about order accuracy and mixed customer reactions.

## BA-relevant concepts

- Human–AI interaction requirements
- Fallback-to-human service paths
- Behavioural outcome metrics (not only efficiency)
- Customer journey design for ordering channels
- Change impact on frontline staff roles

## Practical BA implications (interpretation)

- Define acceptance criteria for comprehension, error handling, and escalation.
- Include behavioural KPIs in pilots (order accuracy, intervention rate).
- Assess brand and health-outcome risks when menus include indulgent options.
- Treat efficiency gains and customer experience as joint success measures.

## Limitations

- Experimental settings; generalisation to all service contexts uncertain.
- Summary paraphrased for portfolio; consult DOI for full methods and findings.
