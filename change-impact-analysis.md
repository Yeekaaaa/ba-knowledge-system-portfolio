# Change Impact Analysis

## Purpose

Assess who and what changes when processes, systems, or automation are introduced — and plan adoption accordingly.

## Inputs

- Current and future process maps — [[process-mapping]]
- Stakeholder map — [[stakeholder-analysis]]
- Transformation KPI themes — [[digital-transformation-framework]]
- Research on workforce impacts (where relevant) — [[public-paper-ai-service-work-summary]]

## Steps

1. Define change scope by **domain**.
2. List impacted stakeholder groups and tasks.
3. Assess impact: skills, workload, controls, relationships, customer experience.
4. Identify risks: adoption failure, shadow processes, employee anxiety, behavioural side effects.
5. Define mitigations: training, communications, pilot, phased rollout, human fallback.
6. Budget change effort (build vs adoption parity per transformation guidance).
7. Define success metrics: value, team health, change progress.

## Outputs

- Change impact assessment
- Training and communications outline
- Adoption checklist
- Risk and mitigation log

## Research-informed cautions (paraphrased)

Voice AI in service settings may affect customer behaviour beyond efficiency metrics; service automation may affect employee perceptions and teamwork. BAs should include **customer and employee** stakeholders in impact analysis. See [[public-paper-ai-service-work-summary]] and [[ai-automation-ba]].

Retail reporting change: store managers adopt new import template; finance owns KPI definitions; regional manager changes monthly review ritual. See `sample-raw/synthetic-project-note.md`.

## Interview phrasing

> I map who changes, how their work changes, and what adoption evidence we need — not only whether the software deployed.
