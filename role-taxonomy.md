## Target role / topic

Business Analyst and adjacent analyst roles — aligned to public Australian job-market signals (sample).

BA titles vary by sector. A taxonomy helps align search, resume keywords, and portfolio stories to market language without copying job ads.

## Role clusters

### Tier 1 — core BA fit

- Business Analyst
- Junior / Graduate Business Analyst
- ICT Business Analyst
- Digital Business Analyst
- Business Systems Analyst
- Process Analyst / Process Optimisation Specialist

### Tier 2 — transformation / change adjacent

- Digital Transformation Analyst
- Business Transformation Analyst
- Change Analyst
- Continuous Improvement Analyst
- Business Operations Analyst

### Tier 3 — data / reporting adjacent

- Data Analyst
- BI / Reporting Analyst
- Commercial / Performance Analyst
- Insights Analyst

## Recurring responsibilities (paraphrased from market signals)

- Bridge business needs and technology solutions
- Elicit and document requirements
- Analyse processes and recommend improvements
- Support transformation or systems initiatives
- Analyse data and produce reporting for stakeholders
- Support UAT and documentation

## Entry-level patterns (sample)

Graduate and junior postings often emphasise:

- Exposure to operations, finance, or supply chain analysis
- Process improvement support
- Data and reporting tasks under supervision
- Cross-functional team collaboration

## Positioning statement (portfolio sample)

> Business Analyst candidate with structured approach to requirements, process mapping, reporting specifications, and testable acceptance criteria — demonstrated on SYNTHETIC sample project in this repository.
