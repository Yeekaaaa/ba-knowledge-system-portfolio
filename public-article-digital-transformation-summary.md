## Key takeaways

- Digital transformation is **continuous organisational rewiring**, not a one-off project.
- Programmes should target **domains** and start from **business problems**, not tools.
- Six capability areas span strategy, talent, operating model, technology, data, and adoption.
- Adoption/change spend should be planned alongside build spend (rule of thumb cited in source).
- Transformation KPIs: value creation, team health, change-management progress.
- Digital transformation should align with **AI transformation** when AI affects decisions.
