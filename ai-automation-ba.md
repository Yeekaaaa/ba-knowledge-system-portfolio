# AI / Automation BA

## Why this domain matters

AI and automation initiatives need BAs who understand workflows, controls, adoption, and **human impacts** — not only model capabilities.

## Core responsibilities

- Map manual and repetitive processes
- Discover automation opportunities — [[ai-automation-opportunity-discovery]]
- Define requirements, controls, exception handling, fallback paths
- Assess customer and employee impacts — [[change-impact-analysis]]
- Support UAT including failure scenarios
- Measure operational and behavioural outcomes

## Research-informed design principles (paraphrased)

| Topic | BA implication |
|---|---|
| Voice AI in ordering | Define comprehension, escalation, behavioural KPIs — [[public-paper-ai-service-work-summary]] |
| Service automation | Include employee stakeholder analysis; helper-not-replacement framing |
| Digital transformation | Problem-first scoping; adoption investment — [[digital-transformation-framework]] |

## Deliverables

- Automation opportunity register
- Use case brief with controls
- Requirements and user stories
- Change impact assessment
- UAT scenarios with human fallback
- Adoption plan

## Interview phrasing

> I approach AI automation as a BA: map how work happens today, define requirements and controls, plan adoption, and measure outcomes beyond efficiency — including customer and employee impacts.
